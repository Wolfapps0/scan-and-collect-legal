Prices, grades and estimates

This is the most important thing to understand about the app:

  • Card recognition can be wrong. Always check that the card the app found is really the card in your hand.
  • Grades are an AI estimate, not a real grade. A grade from the app is not a PSA, BGS or CGC grade, carries no authority, and says nothing about what a grading company would give your card.
  • Prices come from third-party market data (Cardmarket and TCGplayer, via the Pokémon TCG API) and only cover ungraded cards. They move constantly and may be out of date or unavailable. Values you enter yourself are your own estimate.
  • Nothing in the app is financial advice. Don't buy, sell, or insure cards based only on what Scan & Collect shows you.
