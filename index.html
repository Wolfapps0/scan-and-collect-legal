Scan & Collect

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Scan & Collect is a personal card-collecting and grading tool, built and operated by an independent individual developer (not a registered company). This page explains what data the app collects, why, where it goes, and what rights you have over it. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the address at the bottom of this page — we're a one-person team and read every message ourselves.

Data we collect

WhatWhyWhere it's stored
Email address, password Create and sign in to your account Firebase Authentication (Google)
Photos of your cards Identify the card and, optionally, grade its condition Sent to Google Gemini and Ximilar for one-time processing — not stored by us
Your card collection (cards, conditions, grades, quantities) Show your portfolio and its value, and back it up so it survives a lost or reset device Stored on your device, and backed up to Firebase (Google Cloud) under your account so it can be restored on a new device
Card search terms Look up cards and prices Sent to the Pokémon TCG API — no personal data included
We don't run ads, analytics, or trackers in this app, and we don't sell your data to anyone.

Legal basis for processing (EU/EEA users)

We process your email address and password to create and run your account — this is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the app). Photos are processed with your explicit action each time you tap scan or grade — this is also based on performing that contract. We don't process any data based on consent to marketing, because we don't do any.

International data transfers

Some of the services above process data outside the European Economic Area:

  • Firebase and Google Gemini (Google) may process data in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Google is certified under, and contractually bound by, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.
  • Ximilar is based in the Czech Republic (EU), so no transfer outside the EEA occurs for grading.
  • Pokémon TCG API receives only your search text, with no personal data attached.

Your rights

If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK, under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access)
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data (rectification)
  • Ask us to delete your data (erasure) — see "Data retention and deletion" below
  • Ask us to restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority — in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens

To exercise any of these, email us at the address at the bottom of this page. Since most of your data (your collection) already lives on your own device, many of these are as simple as using the app itself; deleting your account also removes your Firestore backup.

If you're in the United States: we do not sell personal information and have no plans to, so there is no "sale" to opt out of. Depending on your state, you may still have a right to know what we collect and to ask us to delete it — see "Data retention and deletion" below, or email us with any request.

Third-party services

Scan & Collect relies on a few external services to work. Each processes the data described above under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — account creation and sign-in
  • Google Gemini API — reading the card name and number from your photos
  • Ximilar — estimating card condition from your photos
  • Pokémon TCG API — card details and market prices

Data retention and deletion

Your collection data lives on your device, and a copy is kept in Firestore (part of Firebase, a Google Cloud service) under your account, purely as a backup so you don't lose your collection if you lose or reset your device. This backup is private to your account — only you, authenticated as yourself, can read or write it; we don't otherwise access, view, or use it. Card photos sent for recognition or grading are processed once and are not retained by us afterward.

To delete your account: open the app, tap "Home" at the top left, and choose "Delete account". This immediately deletes your account, its Firebase Authentication data (email address), and its Firestore backup, and clears your locally saved card collection from the device.

Prefer not to use the app to do it, or can't sign in? Email support.scancollect@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it — we'll delete your account, its Firebase Authentication data, and its Firestore backup within a few business days.

Children's privacy

Scan & Collect is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to use the app, or older if the law of your country or region requires it — some EU countries, including the Netherlands, require you to be 16. If you're under the age of majority where you live, you may only use the app with a parent or guardian's permission and supervision. In line with COPPA, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 anywhere; if we learn that we have, we'll delete it.
