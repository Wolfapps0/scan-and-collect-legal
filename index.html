Scan & Collect

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Scan & Collect is a personal card-collecting and grading tool. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and where it goes. If anything here is unclear, contact us at the address at the bottom of this page.

Data we collect

WhatWhyWhere it's stored
Email address, password Create and sign in to your account Firebase Authentication (Google)
Photos of your cards Identify the card and, optionally, grade its condition Sent to Google Gemini and Ximilar for one-time processing — not stored by us
Your card collection (cards, conditions, grades, quantities) Show your portfolio and its value Stored locally on your device only
Card search terms Look up cards and prices Sent to the Pokémon TCG API — no personal data included
We don't run ads, analytics, or trackers in this app, and we don't sell your data to anyone.

Third-party services

Scan & Collect relies on a few external services to work. Each processes the data described above under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — account creation and sign-in
  • Google Gemini API — reading the card name and number from your photos
  • Ximilar — estimating card condition from your photos
  • Pokémon TCG API — card details and market prices

Data retention and deletion

Your collection data lives only on your device — uninstalling the app removes it. Card photos sent for recognition or grading are processed once and are not retained by us afterward.

To delete your account: open the app, tap "Home" at the top left, and choose "Delete account". This immediately deletes your account and its Firebase Authentication data (email address), and clears your locally saved card collection from the device.

Prefer not to use the app to do it, or can't sign in? Email support.scancollect@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it — we'll delete your account and its Firebase Authentication data within a few business days. Either way, there is no server-side collection data to delete — your card collection is never stored on our servers, only on your device.

Children's privacy

Scan & Collect is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
